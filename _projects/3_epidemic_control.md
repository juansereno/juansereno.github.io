I have worked extensively on the **mathematical modelling and control of epidemic outbreaks**, including optimal social distancing policies and switched control strategies. The central difficulty is balancing public health objectives against socio-economic cost.

Key contributions include methods for minimizing the epidemic final size while constraining the infected peak, and switched NMPC approaches that optimize epidemiological and economic objectives jointly.

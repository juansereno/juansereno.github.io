My current research develops **adaptive digital twins for personalized medicine**, combining mathematical modelling with adaptive estimation. We build iterative calibration methods for medical digital twins using adaptive estimators, so that treatment strategies can be personalized from individual patient data.

The work connects control theory to practical biomedical application, particularly for viral infections and immune system dynamics.

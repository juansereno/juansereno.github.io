### Parameter Monitoring with Adaptive Observers

<p class="post-meta">Short course · Semana IEIC 2026, Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Facultad de Minas · Medellín, Colombia · October 2026 · <a href="https://github.com/juansereno/short-course-adaptive-observers">course materials (GitHub)</a></p>

Instructor. A hands-on short course for undergraduate students on estimating unmeasured states and patient-specific parameters with adaptive observers, the core of a medical digital twin. Using an in-host model of SARS-CoV-2 with a CD8⁺ T-cell response, students go from the model to observability and identifiability, the state-affine form and the observer design, and then code it in MATLAB or Python: from the viral load alone, the observer reconstructs the T cells and learns the patient's parameters as the data arrive. Slides, step-by-step code, Jupyter notebooks and exercises are open on [GitHub](https://github.com/juansereno/short-course-adaptive-observers).
